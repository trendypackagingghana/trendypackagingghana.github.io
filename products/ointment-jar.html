Ointment Jar FAQ

Need something else? Message us on WhatsApp.

What sizes does the ointment jar come in?

The ointment jar is available in 50 ml, 100 ml, 150 ml.

What colours can the ointment jar be made in?

Any colour — we match it to your brand.

What is the minimum order for ointment jars?

The minimum order is 5,000 units and the maximum is 25,000 units per order.

How long does ointment jar production take?

Production typically takes 1–2 weeks depending on quantity. Orders are packed in PE sacks for pickup from our factory in Spintex, Accra.

Can you label the ointment jar?

Yes. We can apply your labels to the ointment jar. We don’t offer printing.
