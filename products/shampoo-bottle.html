Shampoo Bottle FAQ

Need something else? Message us on WhatsApp.

What sizes does the shampoo bottle come in?

The shampoo bottle is available in 250 ml, 500 ml.

What colours can the shampoo bottle be made in?

Any colour — we match it to your brand.

What is the minimum order for shampoo bottles?

The minimum order is 5,000 units and the maximum is 500,000 units per order.

How long does shampoo bottle production take?

Production typically takes 1–2 weeks depending on quantity. Orders are packed in PE sacks for pickup from our factory in Spintex, Accra.

Can you label the shampoo bottle?

Yes. We can apply your labels to the shampoo bottle. We don’t offer printing.
