Quick answers

Lotion Bottle FAQ

Need something else? Message us on WhatsApp.

What sizes does the lotion bottle come in?

The lotion bottle is available in 250 ml, 400 ml, 500 ml.

What colours can the lotion bottle be made in?

Any colour — we match it to your brand.

What is the minimum order for lotion bottles?

The minimum order is 5,000 units and the maximum is 25,000 units per order.

How long does lotion bottle production take?

Production typically takes 1–2 weeks depending on quantity. Orders are packed in PE sacks for pickup from our factory in Spintex, Accra.

Can you label the lotion bottle?

Yes. We can apply your labels to the lotion bottle. We don’t offer printing.
