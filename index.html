How to order

From enquiry to pickup in four steps.

Most bulk orders are ready in 1–2 weeks, depending on quantity.

  1. 1

    Send your enquiry

    Tell us the product, capacity, colour and quantity (5,000–500,000 units).

  2. 2

    Confirm design & material

    Pick a standard shape or approve a custom mould in PE, PP, LDPE or PET.

  3. 3

    Production & labelling

    We manufacture and, if needed, apply your labels. Typical run: 1–2 weeks.

  4. 4

    Collect from Spintex

    Orders are packed in PE sacks, ready for pickup at our factory.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask us.

Can’t find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

Can you make custom plastic packaging for my brand?Custom packaging?

Yes. We design and build custom moulds made exclusively for your brand’s products.

Do you offer printing or labelling?Printing or labelling?

Labelling: yes, for any product. Printing: no, we don’t offer printing services.

What plastics do you use?Which plastics?

We manufacture in PE, PP, LDPE and PET.

What is your minimum and maximum order, and how long does production take?Order size & lead time?

Minimum 5,000 units, maximum 500,000 units. Production typically takes 1–2 weeks depending on order size.

Is your packaging compliant with Ghanaian safety and environmental regulations?Is it compliant?

Yes. Our materials meet all relevant Ghanaian safety and environmental regulations and are food-grade.

Do you deliver to warehouses or stores?Do you deliver?

No. Orders are packed in PE sacks for pickup from our factory in Spintex, Accra.
